# Scope and interpretation

This project summarises a completed **2023 co-authored undergraduate thesis**, not a journal publication, new dataset or validated field study.

**Contains:** project context, literature-based discussion, conceptual comparisons and limitations.

**Does not contain:** original charging measurements, a validated EV scheduling algorithm, verified distribution feeder simulations or released research software.

Please credit **Md. Zahid Hasan and Md. Motiur Rahman** whenever sharing or describing the thesis. The complete PDF is withheld pending sharing permission and privacy review.
