# EV Charging Challenges with Microgrids and Solar Systems in Bangladesh

**Original thesis title:** *Mitigating Electrical Vehicle Charging Challenges with Microgrid and Solar Systems in Bangladesh*  
**Institution:** Daffodil International University, Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering  
**Date:** January 2023  
**Authors:** Md. Zahid Hasan and Md. Motiur Rahman  
**Supervisor:** Md. Rayid Hasan Mojumder

## Overview

This **co-authored BSc thesis** considers EV charging challenges in Bangladesh and discusses solar PV, microgrids and grid interaction as potential approaches to supporting EV charging infrastructure.

## Topics

- EV adoption and charging challenges in Bangladesh
- Solar PV generation and microgrid-supported charging
- Grid-connected operation and vehicle-to-grid (V2G) concepts
- Charging configurations with and without battery storage
- Practical and policy considerations for adoption

## Scope and limitations

The work is primarily **literature-based and conceptual undergraduate research**. It should not be described as a validated experimental study, an advanced feeder simulation or a peer-reviewed journal publication.
